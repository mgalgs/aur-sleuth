This pattern — pulling a build environment and compiling unreviewed code — is a known supply-chain attack vector. The checksums are present but do not mitigate the risk of unreviewed source being compiled.

This behavior is not standard for a simple bootloader entry manager and raises strong suspicion of a hidden payload or backdoor.
