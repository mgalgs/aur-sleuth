While this script is claimed to be for debugging, it runs with elevated privileges inside a container and could be exploited if used in an automated or unattended context.

Combined with the passwordless sudo, this significantly increases the attack surface.

Although the script itself may not directly exfiltrate data or download remote code, it establishes a permissive and insecure environment that undermines container isolation and enables privilege escalation by design. This pattern is commonly abused in supply-chain attacks and violates the principle of least privilege.
