The script's logic is straightforward and legitimate:

None of the operations are suspicious: there are no network requests or downloads, no obfuscation or encoding, no use of eval with untrusted input, no exfiltration of data, and no modification of files outside the scope of restarting Plasma. The D-Bus calls, systemctl --user operations, and iteration through /run/user directories are all standard KDE Plasma session management practices.
