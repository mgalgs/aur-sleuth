How to read this page

These audits look for one thing: code injected into the AUR packaging of a package — the PKGBUILD, an .install hook, or a patch. They do not rate the application itself. Each square is one report: two cheap models audit every package, and a judge — a stronger model — reads the reports when they disagree or flag something.

Clean
nothing found, or a flag a judge overturned.
No verdict
no model reached an answer either way. Missing information, not a suspicion.
Worth a closer look
something flagged it and nothing has settled it. Usually a false positive about behaviour the application is supposed to have; not a claim that it is malicious.
Confirmed malicious
two independent audits said unsafe and a judge agreed.
