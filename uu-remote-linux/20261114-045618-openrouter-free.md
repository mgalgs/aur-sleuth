The environment-variable reads are ordinary configuration (an opt-out flag plus desktop detection), and the lock-state queries use the package's own `crate::session_lock` module. This is upstream remote-desktop functionality (capturing/deferring on a locked screen), not injected supply-chain behavior. The included unit tests reinforce that the behavior is intentional and bounded.
